Emergency Response Workflow

  1. Receive and validate emergency request.
  2. Confirm medical payload with authorized healthcare staff.
  3. Check drone availability, weather and approved flight route.
  4. Assign authorized responder and delivery location.
  5. Verify package handover and temperature records.
